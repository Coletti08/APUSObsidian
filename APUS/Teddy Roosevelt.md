
- Roosevelt was president [[William McKinley]]'s vice president, and after his assassination Roosevelt took over for the rest of his term. 

- For re-election Roosevelt introduced his [[Square Deal]] initiate, which won him re-election by a wide margin.

- When he ran again after his "second term", he ran under his ideas of [[New Nationalism]], which apposed [[Woodrow Willson]]'s [[new Federalism]].
