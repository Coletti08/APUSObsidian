
- A book published by Jacob Rii, documenting the living conditions among immigrants in New York City caused national furor. 
	- Outlines how by 1890 foreign-born immigrants made up 14.7% of the population, up from 9.7 in 1850. 

- Shows the rise of mass amounts of immigration to urban centers during the [[Gilded Age]]. 

- Jacob Rii's book shows the rising popularity of [[Muckraking]]. Which is a giddy display of the difficult living of people during the late 19th century.
