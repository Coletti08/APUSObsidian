

- Part of a number of Acts passed in 1862 including the [[Homestead Act]], [[Pacific Railway Act]], and the [[Morill Act]]. All of these Acts were aimed at modernizing the American Economy. 

- **Purpose**: Create a **national currency** and a system of **national banks** to stabilize the economy and finance the war.
    
- **Impact**: Helped **centralize financial policy**, increased federal control over banking, and funded the **[[Union]] war effort**.
