- Ending the [[Era of Good Feelings]] (1817-1825) after the [[War of 1812]] the country appeared to have entered a period of strength, unity of purpose, and one-party government. 
- Disappearance of [[Federalist]] ideas after the death of Hamilton lead to complete Republican dominance 
- [[Andrew Jackson]] ran agents  [[Henry Clay]](1777-1825), [[William H. Crawford]] (1772-1834), and [[John Quincy Adams]] (1767 - 1848). 
- The election was decided by a combination of electoral and popular votes:
	- Delaware, Georgia, Louisiana, New York, SouthCarolina, and Vermont were all by electoral vote, and all the others were popular Vote. 
- The actual election took more then one day, in the end. Jackson won 99 electoral and 152,901 popular votes, Adams won 84 electoral and 114,000 votes; Crawford won 41 electoral and 47,000 popular votes; and Henry Clay won 37 electoral votes and received 47,000 popular votes. 

- After these original results, [[Henry Clay]] met with [[John Quincy Adams]], and on jan 25, 1825 Clay officially threw his support behind Adams against the non binding agreement that he would support Jackson. 
	- This deal later becomes known as the "Corrupt Bargain".

- This ends the [[Era of Good Feelings]] and begins the battle between the [[Republican]] and formation of the [[Democratic]] by [[Andrew Jackson]].
