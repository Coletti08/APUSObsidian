
- The Sherman Anti-Trust Act is one of the most important pieces of legislation passed in the [[Gilded Age]]. 

- in 1890 congress passes this land slide piece of legislation, that makes it illegal to create shell companies across state lines like Credit Mobilier in the [[Credit Mobilier Scandal]], as well as make it illegal to monopolize or attempt to monopolize any aspect of interstate trade or commerce. 

- Became the basis of new legislation such as :
	- The [[Clayton Antitrust Act]] (1914)
	- The [[Federal Trade Commission Act]] (1914)
