
- [[Henry Clay]], the Republican nominee for president in 1832 proposed rechartering the [[National Bank]]. 
- [[Andrew Jackson]] vetoed the rechartering bill, calling the bank "Unauthorized by the Constitution". 
- The Banks charter expired in 1836, and after the country switched to the use of [State Banks] to manage their money. 
- Although the issue of a national bank remained an issue for two more election cycles.
