**- Key concepts:
    
- British attempts to assert tighter control over its North American colonies, and the colonial resolve to pursue self-government led to a colonial independence movement and the [[American Revolution]] (1775).
    
- The [[American Revolution]] democratic and republican ideals inspired new experiments with different forms of government.
    
- Migration within North America and competition over resources, boundaries, and trade intensified conflicts among peoples and nations.*
