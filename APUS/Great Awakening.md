
- The Great Awakening was inspired by [[Enlightenment Thinking]], in Europe, and it saw the battle between the [[New Light]] and [[Old Light]] sects of the [[Puritans]]. 

- This was the first national experience that everyone in colonial America shared. 

- It contrasted the [[Second Great Awakening]] in that it was a struggle between two sects of the same religion where as the second great awakening was inspired by the [[Travel Revolution]], and saw the freedom of people such as the [[Shakers]] and [[Mormonism]] grow and evolve.
