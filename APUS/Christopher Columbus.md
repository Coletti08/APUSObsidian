- **Christopher Columbus is important in American history** not because he was the first to "discover" the [[New World]], but because his 1492 voyage **initiated lasting European exploration and colonization** of the Western Hemisphere, which fundamentally reshaped global history—including the future United States.

### Key Reasons for His Importance:

#### 1. **Initiated European Colonization**

- His voyage marked the **beginning of sustained contact between Europe and the Americas**, leading to waves of exploration, conquest, and settlement.
    
- Though Columbus landed in the Caribbean, his journey **opened the door for Spain, France, England, and others** to explore and eventually colonize North America.
    

#### 2. **Columbian Exchange**

- His expeditions began the **Columbian Exchange**, a massive transfer of **plants, animals, diseases, and technologies** between the Old World and the New World.
    
- This transformed diets, economies, populations, and environments on both sides of the Atlantic.
    

#### 3. **Changed Global Power Dynamics**

- His voyage helped shift the **center of global power** toward the Atlantic world and accelerated European imperial expansion, directly shaping the emergence of the Americas as key regions in world affairs.
    

#### 4. **Controversial Legacy**

- Columbus is also a **symbol of the beginning of colonization, violence, and displacement** of Indigenous peoples.
    
- In recent decades, many have re-evaluated his legacy, leading to debates over **Columbus Day** and the push for **Indigenous Peoples’ Day** in many parts of the U.S.
