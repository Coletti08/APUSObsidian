**- Anti-Federalists:
    

- Believed in state sovereignty.
    
- At a steep disadvantage organizationally. 
    
- Knew the confederation wasn’t working ([[Shays Rebellion]]), but didn’t want as strong of a centralized government as the Constitution invoked. 
    
- Suggested that the Federalists were working in secret to undermine the American people. 
    
- Didn't create a bill of rights, because they used the states rights instead. This was a mistake, because the federalists used this to their advantage.**
