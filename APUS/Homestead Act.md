
- [[Abraham Lincoln]] signed the act, that granted public land in 160 acres allotments in the West to those willing to farm the land for five years. 

- The first of four acts including the [[Pacific Railway Act]] (1862),  the [[Morill Act]]  (1862), and the [[National Banks Act]] (1863) which were all aimed at modernizing the [Federal Governments] role in shaping the economy and land. 


- **Purpose**: Encourage settlement of the West by offering **160 acres of free land** to settlers who lived on and improved it for 5 years.
    
- **Impact**: Expanded the idea of **free labor** and independent farming, and accelerated **westward migration**.
