
- Part of Monroe's 1823 annual letter to congress, warning European powers not to interfere with the affairs of the Western Hemisphere 
	- This effected Britain too, as they had setup profitable trade routes with Latin America. 
	- There would be problems if Spain regained its [[New World]] colonies, and had claims to territory in the Oregon country of the Pacific Northwest.
	- To combat this the America and Britain launched a joint announcement 

- [[John Quincy Adams]] secretly opposed the "joint announcement" of the Monroe Doctrine, and convinced Monroe to declare the solely American policy of the "Monroe Doctrine"

- This new Doctrine declared that "pupped governments" and further colonization. In exchange America wouldn't interfere with internal European affairs. 

- The doctrine was enforced by the Royal Navy, as America lacked the naval capabilities to defend a European invasion.
