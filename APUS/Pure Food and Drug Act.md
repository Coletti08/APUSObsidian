
- The **Pure Food and Drug Act of 1906** was a landmark piece of [[Progressive]] Era legislation that aimed to **protect consumers** by regulating the labeling and quality of food and medicine in the United States. It was the **first federal law** to address **food and drug safety**, and it laid the foundation for the creation of the [[Food and Drug Association]] (FDA).**

- released due to the impact of Upton Sinclair's book [[The Jungle]].
