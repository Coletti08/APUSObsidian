
- Founded in Tennessee, in 1865 by Thomas M. Jones. 
- The **Ku Klux Klan (KKK)** was highly significant during [[Reconstruction]] (1865–1877) because it played a central role in **undermining efforts to rebuild the South on the basis of racial equality** and democracy. The Klan used **terrorism and violence** to suppress the political and civil rights of newly freed African Americans and their white allies.

- **Federal Response**:
    
    - The U.S. government passed the [[Enforcement Acts]] (1870–71) to fight Klan violence
        
    - President [[Ulysses S. Grant]] used federal troops and legal action to temporarily crush the Klan
