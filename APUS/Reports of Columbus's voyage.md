
- 1493, [[Christopher Columbus]] sent a letter to King Ferdinand describing his voyage across the Atlantic.
- Columbus explored the Caribbean islands, specifically Juana (Cuba), and Hispaniola (Santo Domingo) before returning to Spain. 
- He left men to build a settlement called [La Navidad] on president day Haiti. 
- The letter was sent to Rome where it was printed in Latin with out the inclusion of Isabella's name.
