Here's a more detailed look at the key Treaty of Paris:

1. Treaty of Paris (1783):

- **Purpose:** Officially ended [[The Revolutionary War]] 
- **Significance:** Formally recognized the independence of the United States from Great Britain and established the boundaries of the new nation. 

- **Key provisions:**

    - British recognition of U.S. independence. 
    - Defined the borders of the United States, including westward expansion. 
        - Fishing rights on the Newfoundland banks were granted to the United States. 
    - Pre-war debts owed to British creditors were addressed. 
    - Restitution of property lost during the war was agreed upon. 
    - British forces were to evacuate the thirteen states. 
    

1. Treaty of Paris (1763):

- **Purpose:**
    
    Ended the French and Indian War (also known as the Seven Years' War) between Great Britain and France.
    

- **Significance:**
    
    France ceded its mainland North American territories to Great Britain, effectively ending any foreign military threat to the British colonies. 
    

1. Treaty of Paris (1898):

- **Purpose:** Ended the [[Spanish-American War]]. 

- **Significance:** Spain ceded Cuba, Puerto Rico, and Guam to the United States. 

- **Key provisions:**
    
    - Spain relinquished sovereignty over Cuba. 
    
    - The United States took possession of Cuba, Puerto Rico, and Guam. 
    
    - The United States purchased the Philippines from Spain for $20 million.
