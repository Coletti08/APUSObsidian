
- One of the largest strikes of the [[Gilded Age]], the strike lasted several months. 
- Began when Henry C. Frick, the general manager of [[Andrew Carnegie]]'s steel plant in Homestead, Pennsylvania, cut wages and attempted to quash workers Union attempting to protest. 
- Frick closed the plant and payed a private militia to come and quash the strike. 
- The ensuing violence forced the Pennsylvania state militia to come and quell the altercation. 
- All in all this was a major loss for labor unions all across America.
