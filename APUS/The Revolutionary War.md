
- The Revolutionary war (1775 - 1783):

- The war was the culmination of building tensions after the [[Boston Massacre]], [[The Tea Act]], the [[Sugar Act]], and the [[Stamp Act]]. 

- At the start of the war they were fighting for their right as Englishmen until common sense when the Americans realize that they should control ourselves. 

- The American Revolution—also called the U.S. War of Independence—was **the insurrection fought between 1775 and 1783 through which 13 of Great Britain's North American colonies threw off British rule to establish the sovereign United States of America**, founded with the Declaration of Independence in 1776
