Key aspects of the Tariff of 1832:

- **Purpose:**
    
    The tariff aimed to lower tariffs slightly compared to the 1828 tariff, known as the "Tariff of Abominations," while still maintaining protectionist measures to support Northern industries. 

- **Southern Opposition:**
    
    While a reduction, the tariff was still seen as unfair by Southern states, particularly South Carolina, which relied heavily on agriculture and exports. 
    

- Directly lead to the  [[Nullification Crisis]] of 1832, and resolved by the [[Compromise Tariff of 1833]]. 
    

- **Nullification doctrine:**
    
    The Nullification Crisis highlighted the debate surrounding the doctrine of nullification, which asserted that states could nullify federal laws within their borders.
