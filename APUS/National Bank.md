- created in 1790 - 1836 by Hamilton
- The National Bank became a private partner to the Federal Government. 

- Creation:
	- [[Alexander Hamilton]] proposed the **Bank of the United States** to manage government funds, issue a national currency, and provide loans to promote commerce and industry.
    
	- It was modeled on the Bank of England and passed in 1791 despite opposition from Jefferson and Madison.

- A singular "National Bank" was removed by [[Andrew Jackson]] after the [[Bank War]] (1832). After it was dissolved Jackson created [State Banks].
