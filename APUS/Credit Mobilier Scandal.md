
- Credit Mobilier was created by [[The Union Pacific]]  stockholders such as [Thomas Durant]. 
- Credit Mobilier was created as Union Pacific's own private construction company to help them finish the [[Trans Continental Railroad]]. 

- The scandal part of Credit Mobilier comes from all the stockholders pouring both generous government subsidies and much of the financials from Union Pacific into Credit Mobilier, Union Pacific was bankrupted by this, and many people were left with worthless securities. 
	- Eleven members of Congress who were accused of accepting shares of Credit Mobilier stock.
