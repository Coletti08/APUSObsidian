
- McKinley was the 25th president of the [[United States]] from (1897-1901). 
- McKinley was played a vital role in the US's transition to an [imperialistic] nation. 

- He introduced the [[McKinley Tariff]] which was detrimental to the nations economy in the late 1880's. 
- He brokered the [[1898 Treaty of Paris]], which granted us [Guam, Puerto Rico, and the Philippines]. 
- He signed the [[Gold Standard]] in 1900, which ended the debate around a default. 
- created the [[Open Door Policy]], which helped the US expand into neighboring markets, and contributed to Jacksons [[Frontier Thesis]]. 

- in Sept 1901, McKinley was shot twice by Leon Czolgosz, who was an American [[Anarchists]].
