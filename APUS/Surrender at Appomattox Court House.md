
- By April of 1865, general [[Ulysses S. Grant]] was able to cut off [[Robert E. Lee]]'s supplies forcing the [[Confederacy]] to evacuate Petersburg and Richmond. 
- Lee and his men retreated westward, but Grant's troops over took him about a hundred miles west of Richmond.
- This marked the end of the war for Lee, and on April 9th Robert E, Lee surrendered.
