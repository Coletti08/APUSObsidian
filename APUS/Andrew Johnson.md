
- Vice president under [[Abraham Lincoln]], Johnson was chosen for his southern leanings which Lincoln hoped would make him appealing to former [[Confederacy]] members. Instead after Lincoln was killed Johnson took office, and began his crusade against [[Reconstruction]]. 

- Johnson Vetoed twenty Reconstruction bills and urged southern legislators to reject the [[fourteenth Amendment]]. 
- The house voted 126 - 47 to impeach Johnson on eleven separate articles.
