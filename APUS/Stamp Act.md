**- Stamp Act (1765):

- one year after the [[Sugar Act]] (1764)
- Completely enforced for the reason of Revenue. 
- Levied new taxes (sales tax) on pamphlets, almanacs, newspapers, playing cards, dice, and a wide range of documents. 
    
- This act was forced upon them with almost no colonial intervention or say. 
    
- August 14, 1765. Massachusetts patriots forced the stamp collector, Andrew Oliver to resign his office, effectively ending the stamp act in Massachusetts. 
    
- Enacted to pay for British troops stationed during the [[Seven Years' War]].
    
- Required colonists to play a tax, represented by a stamp, on various forms of papers, documents, and playing cards.**

- The stamp act was repealed, but they were told the colonsits that they could be taxed in the future.
