
- The discovery of gold in California on January 24th, 1848 lead to swarms of men coming to California from all around the world. 
	- In 1849, 80,000 men arrived in California hoping to make fortune mining. 
- Although many hoped to strike it big very few actually got rich from the gold, which lasted less then a decade.

- However, the population boom allowed California to **skip territorial status** and apply for **statehood in 1850**.
    
- California's admission as a **free state** tipped the delicate balance in Congress, leading to the [[Compromise of 1850]].

The Gold rush also accelerated [[American Expansion]]:
- Reinforced the idea of [[Manifest Destiny]]—that the U.S. was destined to expand coast to coast.
    
- Encouraged the [[Travel Revolution]] to expand railroads and telegraph lines into the west.
