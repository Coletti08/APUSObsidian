
- A book created by Harriet Beecher Stow in 1852. right after the [[Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo]]. 
- Became the best selling book in America.

- The novel follows the story of Uncle Tom, a deeply religious enslaved man, and the lives of others around him who suffer under slavery.
    
- It portrays a range of characters—both enslaved people and slaveholders—to show the **inhumanity and moral corruption** of the institution of slavery.
    
- Stowe, a white abolitionist from the North, wrote the book in response to the [[Fugitive Slave Act]] (1850), which forced free states to return escaped slaves.
