
- Helen Hunt Jackson exposes the federal governments's harsh and duplicitous treatment of American Indians in the West. 
- In her book Jackson exposes the horrors of the [[Trail of Tears]], [[Indian Removal Act]], [[Indian Appropriation Act]], [[Nez Perce War]]. Exposing how poorly the Federal government has treated Native Americans throughout the years. 

- Her book helped at least a little bit, as congress soon passed the [[Dawes Severalty Act]] of 1887.
