- **Alien Enemies Act:**
    
    This act empowered the president to detain or deport male aliens, citizens of a country at war with the US, during wartime. 
    

- **Naturalization Act:**
    
    This law extended the residency requirement for US citizenship from 5 to 14 years, aiming to restrict the influx of new immigrants who might favor the opposition Democratic-Republican party. 
    

- **Alien Friends Act the president to deport any non-citizen deemed dangerous to the peace, even if the US was not at war. 
    

- **Sedition Act:**
    
    This law criminalized the publication of false, scandalous, or malicious writings against the US government, effectively stifling political dissent and criticism. 
    

The Alien and Sedition Acts sparked significant controversy and public outrage, particularly among Democratic-Republicans who saw them as a violation of First Amendment rights and a tool to suppress political opposition. These acts contributed to the Federalist Party's political decline and their defeat in the 1800 election, where Thomas Jefferson, the Democratic-Republican candidate, won the presidency
