
#### . **Support for Free Silver**

- Bryan argued that the U.S. should **coin silver freely at a 16:1 ratio to gold**, which would increase the money supply.
    
- This would cause **mild inflation**, helping debt-ridden **farmers and laborers** by making loans easier to repay and raising crop prices.
    

#### 2. **Opposition to the Gold Standard**

- Bryan believed the gold standard benefitted **bankers, bondholders, and the industrial elite**, while harming ordinary Americans.
    
- He criticized Eastern financial interests for sacrificing the working class to maintain economic control.
    

#### 3. **Iconic Final Line:**

> “**You shall not press down upon the brow of labor this crown of thorns, you shall not crucify mankind upon a cross of gold.**”

This metaphor equated the gold standard to a form of economic **persecution and suffering**, likening it to the **crucifixion of Christ**.

---
**Significance of the Speech:**

- Made Bryan the **Democratic presidential nominee** in 1896 at age 36.
    
- Energized the **Populist and agrarian movements**, who had long advocated for monetary reform.
    
- Became one of the **most famous political speeches in U.S. history**, symbolizing the clash between **rural populism and industrial capitalism**.
