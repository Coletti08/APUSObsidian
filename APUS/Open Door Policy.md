
- Fearful of a weakened non [[Communist]] China being divided among European economic interests, the US pushed for equal trading rights for all nations in China. 

- The policy helped America grow its sphere of influence in the global economy, and the economic interests of China, as the beginnings of the [[First Red Scare]] started to take hold. 

- The U.S. asked major powers (Britain, France, Germany, Russia, Japan, and Italy) to agree that:

1. All nations should have **equal access to Chinese ports and trade**.
    
2. No power should gain **exclusive control over any part of China**.
    
3. China’s **territorial and administrative integrity** should be respected.
