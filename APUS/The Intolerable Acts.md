**- Coercive Acts (intolerable Acts) 1773.
    
- Nullification of [[Massachusetts Charter]] of 1691
    
- Allowed royal officials accused of committing felonies while executing their offices in Massachusetts tried in England. 
    
- [[Quartering Act]], facilitated housing troops where they could be used against colonial civilians. 
    

- Constitutional congress met in Philadelphia on sept 5 - oct 26, 1774, and adapted the statement of rights, demanding the repeal of the Coercive Acts. 
    
- Parliament was not the focus of American disengagement, but the king.**
