
- In 1877, Railroad workers in Martins burg, West Virginia, insisted a strike to protest  the working conditions and wage cuts. 
- The Strike spread quickly and lasted more then a month, leading some to call it the "Great labor uprising" 
- Violent confrontations broke out in many cities across the country, Virginia even called out their state militias. 

- This is important because it demonstrates the ever rising tensions between the working class and the people profiting off of their hard work.
