

- The proclamation Given in 1863 by [[Abraham Lincoln]]that declared all enslaved people freed. 
	- This didn't automatically make slaves in the [[Confederacy]] free, but it did ensure that once the war was over all enslaved peoples would become freed. 
- It is also important to note that the proclamation was also used to announce the inclusion of African Americans in the [[Union]] Army with the [[54th Massachusetts Regiment]].  

- This proclamation came before the [[Battle of Gettysburg]], so it was scene as an over step of Lincoln's boundaries as president.
