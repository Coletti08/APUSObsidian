**- The proclamation of 1763:
    

- At the end of the [[Seven Years' War]] (1754-1763)
    
- France surrendered Canada and much of the Ohip and Mississippi river Valleys to England. 
    
- *Proclamation decrees that all land west of the Appalachian Mountains were off limits to white settlers. 
    
- restricted commerce with American Indians and traders licensed by the British. 
    
- The British government hoped that by restricting the settlers freedom to move around they would get into less costly wars, and protect the fur trade.
    
- To verify that their demands were met, the British sent 10,000 troops along the frontier.**
