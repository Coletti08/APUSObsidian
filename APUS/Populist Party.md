
- the populist, or peoples party was formed by regular people. mostly farmers and laborers, who desired change. began the [[Populist Movement]]. 

- The populist party convention was held on July 2, 1892 where they outlined their need for a [Graduate Income Tax] a "flexible" currency based both a [[Silver Standard]] and [[Gold Standard]] The Normalization of railroads and the telegraph, an eight our work day and bans on "aliens" owning land


- Responsible for policies regarding subsidies the protection of union organizers, and the [[Sixteenth Amendment]], which formed a graduated income tax.
