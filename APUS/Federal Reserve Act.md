
- Created the federal reserve system to serve as the nations [Central Bank]. Established twelve regional reserve banks that were a part of this larger system. 

- This act lead to the reduction of power of "wall street" and private bankers. 

- The act was established by [[Woodrow Willson]] in an attempt put an end to financial crisis such as [[Panic of 1893]], or the [[Panic of 1873]], or even the [[panic of 1907]]. 

- However this system wouldn't prevent the events of [[Black Tuesday]] and the crash of the stock market during the [[Great Depression]].
