
- The first all-black regiment formed in the [[Union]] following [[Abraham Lincoln]]'s [[Emancipation Proclamation]]. 

- The regiment began its assault on [Fort Wagner] a [[Confederacy]] strong hold on July 18, 1863.

- After the [[Civil War]] sergeant William Harvey Carney became the first African American to be awarded with a Congressional Medal of Honor for taking up the fallen Union flag and caring it to the forts walls.
