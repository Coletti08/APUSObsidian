
- Also known as the Genera; Allotment Act. 
- Allowed Indian reservation land to be broken up into small allotments for sale to individuals. 
- The purpose of this act was to encourage American Indians to become farmers, but the plots were to small to support families or to raise livestock. 

- This act is good in theory because it is supposed to help American Indians have more control over the land they live on. 
	- This was not the case as many American Indians did not receive land as a result of this act. 

- One of many acts meant to accelerate the assimilation of Native Americans into American life. 
	- Along with the [[Indian Appropriation Act]]
