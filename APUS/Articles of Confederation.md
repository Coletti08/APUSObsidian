- There is no power greater then the states.
- Gave the federal government the ability to:
	- print money
	- construct a military
	- create foreign policy 
	- [[American Expansion]]
- However, the real power lay in the states. 
- Cannot pass a law under the Articles of Confederation with out unanimous agreement from the states. 
- One of the greatest achievements of the Articles of Confederation was the creation of the [[Continental Army]]. 

- The Articles of Confederation (Dec 16 1777):
- Neither a [[Federalist]] or [[Anti-federalist]] agenda. 
    
- At the end of the conference a draft of the Articles of Confederation was sent to each state for ratification. 
    
- Virginia was first, Maryland was last. 
    
- Didn’t infringe upon the sovereignty of each of the 13 states, but looked to unionize them. 
    
- Under the articles states had the power to level taxes not congress. 
    
- Didn’t establish a genuinely republican government, power was concentrated in a single assembly, rather than being divided. As in, state governments, into houses and branches. (didn’t want to match the structure in Britain)
    
- The Articles were the plan of governorship until [[The Constitution]] in 1788.

- announced the [[Land Ordinance]], and the [[North West Ordinance]].

- In some ways the Articles were successful, however it was never ment to last, and was always meant to be replaced.
