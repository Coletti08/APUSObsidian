
- Passed in 1883 in an attempt to curb corruption and patronage, the act introduced federal exams and merit requirements for civil servants. 

- The pendelton Act ended the [[Spoils System]], which was being abused by both [[Democrats]] and [[Republican]]s during [[Reconstruction]]. 
	- Was especially popular after the many scandals such as [[Whisky Ring Scandal]]. 

- The Act was in direct response to the assassination of president [[James Garfield]] who was killed after he refused to grant a position to one such office seeker.
