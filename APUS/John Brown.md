**2. Harpers Ferry Raid (1859)
- Lead an armed abolitionist raid on [[Harpers Ferry]] Two days later the US Army, lead by [[Robert E. Lee]] stormed Harpers Ferry and captured Brown
- 

- Brown led a raid on a federal arsenal in Harpers Ferry, Virginia
- His goal was to spark a massive slave uprising by arming enslaved people
- The raid failed; he was captured by U.S. Marines led by Robert E. Lee, tried for treason, and executed by hanging
