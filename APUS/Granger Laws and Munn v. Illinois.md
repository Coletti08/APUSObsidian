
- Granger Laws were created by [[Grange]] meetings in each town community, together they came up with these laws as the idea that they should be able to get more through the railroads. 

- Granger Laws was a coalition of farmers founded in 1867 with the common goal to advance the methods of agriculture, promote the social and economic needs of farmers in the United States. At both the state and National level. 

- The court case and subsequent [Supreme Court] case were early attempts of agrarian's to limit big business. 


- **Granger Laws**: Early efforts by farmers to use state laws to regulate powerful businesses like railroads and grain warehouses.
    
- **Munn v. Illinois**: A landmark Supreme Court case that **upheld the constitutionality of those laws**, establishing the principle that **private business can be regulated when it serves the public interest**.
