
- Car company workers initiate a boycott of pullman train cars through American Railway Union. They boycott, observed by 150,000 members of the AMR Union, stopped rail traffic in and out of Chicago and affected rail traffic across the country. 
- [[Grover Cleveland]] sent federal forces to protect trains being run by non-union strikebreakers. 
- Pro-union mobs destroyed trains and buildings leading to the jailing of union and strike leaders and the end of the American Railway [[Unions]].
