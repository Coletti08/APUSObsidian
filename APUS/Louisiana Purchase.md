- 1803, Jeffersons First term

- A move away from [[Thomas Jefferson]]'s [[Anti-federalist]] stance. 

- Spain secretly ceded the Louisiana territory. The area stretching from Canada to the Gulf Coast and from Mississippi River to the Rocky Mountains. 

- To France, which then closed the port of New. Orleans to American farmers. Jefferson fearing that a French colonial empire in North American would block American expansion, sent negotiators with instructions to buy New Orleans and much of the Gulf Coast as they could for 10 million. 

-  After the purchase Jefferson sent [[Lewis and Clark]] to learn about this new territory. Their expedition returned to Missouri in sept 1806 with valuable maps.
