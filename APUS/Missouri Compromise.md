
- The purpose of the Missouri Compromise was to settle which states would be considered "Slave States". this worked well until the [[Kansas - Nebraska Act]], which put the fate of slave states up to a vote, un knowingly causing  [[Bleeding Kansas]]. 
- In 1819 a courageous group of Northern congressmen and senators opened a debate on the most divisive of post war political issues. --Slavery--. 
- The movement was ultimately a failure 
- The movement was lead by the [[Federalist]] Rufus King. 
- Divided slave states from non slave states across the "36°, 30° N" line. Below was slave, and above was free. 
	- Missouri was accepted to the Union as a free state despite this line. 
	- Maine was split off from Massachusetts in order to balance out the seats in congress.
