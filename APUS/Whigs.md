
- Created in the wake of [[Andrew Jackson]]'s abuse of presidential power, a coalition of "Anti-Jackson" political parties merged to form the Whig Party. 

- [[Henry Clay]] became a Whig. 

- [[Democratic]] voters tended to be small farmers, residents of less prosperous towns, and Scots-Irish or Catholic Irish. 

-  Whig voters tended to be: educators, professionals, manufacturers, business- oriented farmers, British and German Protestant immigrants, manual laborers, free blacks and active members of Presbyterian, Unitarian and Congregational churches.


- Collapsed in the 1850's due to [[Bleeding Kansas]] and the debate over Slavery, became the northern and southern whigs.
