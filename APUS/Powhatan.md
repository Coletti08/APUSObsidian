

-  The tribal leader of the "Powhatan" tribe, most well known for their dealings with the early [[James Town]] colony. 
- The Powhatan were a tribe of warriors, and they had many altercations with the colonists.
- [[Pocahontas]] daughter of Powhatan famously saved one of the Englishmen [[John Smith]] from death, although the degree of her involvement in this altercation is probably exaggerated.
