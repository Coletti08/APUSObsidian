
- extended the [[Monroe Doctrine]], which asserted the right of the United States to police the Caribbean. 

- Declared that the **U.S. had the right to intervene in Latin American countries** to stabilize them economically and politically if they were unable to pay debts or maintain order.
- Justified this intervention as a way to **prevent European powers** from using military force in the region.


- In nature the Roosevelt Corollary was "pro Imperialistic", as it justified US intervention such as the [[Panama Canal]], and the [[Spanish-American War]].


- #### **Expanded U.S. Influence**

- Transformed the U.S. into the **dominant power in the Western Hemisphere**.
- Led to repeated U.S. interventions in countries like **Cuba, Haiti, the Dominican Republic, and Nicaragua**.
    

#### 2. **Justified "[[Big Stick Politics]]"**

- Reflected Roosevelt’s foreign policy: **“Speak softly and carry a big stick.”**
- The U.S. would act as an **"international police power"** in the Americas.
#### 3. **Set a Precedent for Future U.S. Intervention**

- Used to justify military and economic involvement in Latin America for decades.
- It was both praised for promoting stability and **criticized as imperialistic**.
