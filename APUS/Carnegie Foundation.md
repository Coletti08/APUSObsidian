
#### 1. **Carnegie Corporation of New York (1911)**

- **Flagship foundation** created to promote the “advancement and diffusion of knowledge and understanding.”
    
- Funded public libraries, education reform, teacher training, and later civil rights and international peace efforts.
    
- Continues today as a major philanthropic foundation.
    

#### 2. **Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching (1905)**

- Aimed to improve **teacher training**, **educational standards**, and **academic pensions**.
    
- Created the **TIAA** pension system, which still serves educators and nonprofits today.
    
- Played a major role in shaping U.S. higher education policies and accreditation systems.
    

#### 3. **Carnegie Endowment for International Peace (1910)**

- Focused on promoting **global peace and diplomacy**.
    
- Still active today in international affairs and policy research.
