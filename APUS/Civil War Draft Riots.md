
- kicked off by the [[Conscription Act]] (March 2, 1863), the Draft Riots took place in New York, where a mob of angry Irish Catholic immigrants attacked black citizens and institutions in NY. 
	- Lead to one of the worst demonstrations of mob violence in American history. 
	- 120 people were killed, dozens of African American men were lynched
	- Draft offices, black neighborhoods, and homes of leading Republican s and abolitionists were burned.
