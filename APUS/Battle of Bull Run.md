
- [[Union]] Armies were defeated at the First Major battle of the [[Civil War]]. At Bull Run (Manassas) in Virginia, both [[Union]] and [[Confederacy]] armies consisted of poorly trained volunteers. 
	- This disastrous encounter forced the Union to realize that the fight to hold the [[United States]] Together would not be easily or swiftly won. 

- Showed how difficult the [[Civil War]] would be. Union considered the war to be trivial and would soon be over, but this was not the case, and the  [[Attack on Fort Sumter]]  in April and the battle of bull run now proved this fact.
