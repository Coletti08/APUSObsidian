- Founder of the [[Massachusetts Bay Colony]], Winthrop was on the [[Mayflower]]. 
- Delivered the famous "City on a Hill" Speech:
	- Winthrop told the [[Puritans]] that their new colony would be watched by the world as a **moral example**, saying:

> “**We shall be as a city upon a hill. The eyes of all people are upon us.**”

This line echoes **Biblical language** from **Matthew 5:14**, where Jesus says:

> “You are the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hidden.”


- **Moral Responsibility**  
    The Puritans had a covenant with God to live in a just, godly society. If they failed, they would not only be punished by God but also **shamed before the world**.
    
- **Community and Charity**  
    Winthrop emphasized **unity**, **mutual care**, and **sacrifice** for the common good—especially among the wealthy and poor.
    
- **Religious Idealism**  
    The colony was meant to be **God’s example on Earth**, a shining beacon of Christian values and governance.
