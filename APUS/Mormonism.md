
- Began during the [[Second Great Awakening]] (C. 1830) in upstate New York by Joseph Smith, and the [Church of Latter-Day Saints]. 

- Moved to Utah in 1844. 

- Advocated for polygamy, and because of it they were pushed out of several communities. 

- Smith was killed in the Illinois jail in 1844 by an angry mob. 

- Now under the leadership of Brighten Young, mormons emigrated to the Salt Lake Valley to establish the religious state of  Deseret.
