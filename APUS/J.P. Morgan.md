
- America's most successful investment banker. Morgan found success in reorganizing railroads and consolidating other industries, and in finance and insurance. 
- In 1892 Morgan merged Edison General Electric and Thomson-Houston Electric Company to form [[General Electric]]
- After financing the creation of the [Federal Steel Company], he merged it in 1901 with [[Andrew Carnegie]]'s [[US Steel]] corporation.


- During the [[Panic of 1893]] as well as the [[panic of 1907]], Morgan rescued the American Economy from collapse. 
	- In 1907, he **personally organized a bailout** of failing banks and the New York Stock Exchange, preventing a full economic collapse.
	- His role showed that **private finance** was more powerful than the federal government at the time.
