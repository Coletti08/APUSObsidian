
- Almost the first World War, as it was set across multiple continents.  

- Also known as  The French and Indian War 1755)
- Lasted from 1754 to 1763.

- The French and British colonies along with the Native Americans fought for the limited land and resources east of the Mississippi.  
    
- Britain claimed they had stake over the east coast because they were “there the longest”
    
- Lead to an intense national feeling WITH Britain, they epitomize all the good qualities of the British. (fades quickly after this)

- After the war Native Americans felt very exposed with no more European powers to pit against each other. So they started [[Pontiac Rebellion]] (1763).
