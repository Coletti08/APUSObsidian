
- The voyage from Africa to America, more than any other Migration the Middle Passage has come to epitomize the experiences of people of African descent throughout the Atlantic World.
- The nightmarish weeks and sometimes months locked in holds of stinking slave ships represent the:
	- Loss of freedom
	- Degradation of enslavement
	- Dehumanization
	- The long years of bondage that followed
- Also represents the will to survive, and the strength of the African people.
