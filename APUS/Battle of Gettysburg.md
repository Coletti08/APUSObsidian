
- forces of the [[Confederacy]] lead by general [[Robert E. Lee]] faced off against [[Union]] General [George Mead], Lee tried to attack Union positions from the left and right flanks, but was repelled. 
	- This lead to general [[George Pickett]] to lead some 15,000 Confederate troops into Union fire. 
- When Lee ordered a retreat, it was clear that the Confederacy had suffered a disastrous defeat. At the cost of around 51,000 men.

-  **urned the tide of the war** by halting Lee’s invasion of the North
    
- **Major Confederate defeat** with heavy, irreplaceable losses
    
- **Boosted Union morale** alongside the victory at Vicksburg
    
- **Ended Confederate hopes** for foreign recognition or Northern surrender
    
- **Inspired Lincoln’s Gettysburg Address**, reframing the war as a fight for equality and democracy
    
- **Marked a shift** toward total war and relentless Union strategy
