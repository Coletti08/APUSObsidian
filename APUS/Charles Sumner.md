
- After Sumner delivered a speech where he denounced the number of southern senators for supporting slavery, the highly abolitionist senator Charles Sumner was assaulted by Representative Preston Brooks, who used a cane to beat Sumner.
	- Preston Brooks (Bully Brooks as he would come to be christened) became a southern hero, he represented [Southern Honor] and [Sectional Pride]. 
	- In the north Sumner became a martyr to the causes of freedom. Millions of his speech were printed and spread throughout the north. 
- The Brooks incident only furthered the divide between North and South.
