
- Mark Twain is the author of "The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn" which is regarded as one of the most important works of American literature of all time. 

- Huck finn, published during the [[Gilded Age]] and the rise of [[Jim Crow]] South discusses a white boy's relationship with a black man challenging race roles of the time. 

- Praised by authors like **Ernest Hemingway**, who said:
	“All modern American literature comes from _Huckleberry Finn_.”
