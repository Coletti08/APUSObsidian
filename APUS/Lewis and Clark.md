- **Purpose:**
    
    To explore and map the ;[[Louisiana Purchase]], seeking a Northwest Passage, establishing diplomatic relations with Native American tribes, and claiming the land for the United States. 
    
- **Route:**
    
    The expedition followed the Missouri River and its tributaries westward, eventually reaching the Pacific Ocean in 1805, as recorded in their journals. 
    

- **Native American Encounters:**
    
    They encountered numerous Native American tribes, including the Shoshone, Mandan, and Blackfeet, establishing diplomatic relations and trading with them, often through the use of Jefferson Indian Peace Medals.

- **Accomplishments:**
    
    The expedition succeeded in reaching the Pacific, mapping the land, establishing American presence, and gaining valuable knowledge about the geography, flora, fauna, and Native American cultures of the region, according to Wikipedia.
