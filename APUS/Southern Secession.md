

- After the [[Election of 1860]] Southerners feared that Lincoln would end slavery. 
	- South Carolina becomes the first state to secede from the [[Union]], quickly followed by Mississippi, Florida, Alabama, Georgia, and Louisiana by Jan 1861.
	
- Southern States established a new governing body under the [[Confederacy]] and drafted the [[Confederacy Constitution]], although molded after the US [[Constitution]] the Confederate document specifically referred to:
	- Slavery
	- State sovereignty
	- And God
- The Confederate Constitution explicitly guaranteed slavery in the states and territories and prohibited the [[Slave Trade]].
