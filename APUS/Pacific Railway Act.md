
- Part of a number of Acts passed in 1862 including the [[Homestead Act]], [[Morill Act]], and the [[National Banks Act]]. All of these Acts were aimed at modernizing the American Economy. 

- **Purpose**: Fund the construction of the [[Trans Continental Railroad]] by granting land and loans to companies.
    
- **Impact**: Connected the East and West coasts, enabled troop and resource movement during the [[Civil War]], and laid the groundwork for a **national market economy**.
