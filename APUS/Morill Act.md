
- Part of a number of Acts passed in 1862 including the [[Homestead Act]], [[Pacific Railway Act]], and the [[National Banks Act]]. All of these Acts were aimed at modernizing the American Economy. 

- **Purpose**: Provide federal land to states to fund the creation of **colleges focused on agriculture and the mechanical arts**.
    
- **Impact**: Strengthened **education and innovation**, especially in farming and technology, supporting economic development.
