	
- The 1880's represented a monumental shift for labor [[Unions]]. As working conditions continued to decline people began to go on strike leading to the "Great strikes" of the 1880's. 
	- The [[Great Railway Strike of 1877]], and the [[The Haymarket Square Riot]] are good examples of people rebelling against large companies and the police. 
- The creation of "May Day" or [[Labor Day]] was created during this time period as a response to poor working conditions. 
	- The day too represents a monumental win for labor unions across America.
