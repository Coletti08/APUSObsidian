- Caused by the [[Panic of 1873]]. 

- because of the [[Greenback]] system that was employed during the civil war to pay for things. 

- This is a very important act, because it returned the US to the [[Gold Standard]] after the [[Civil War]], which became a contested part of the [[Populist Movement]].  



- 
    **Return to the Gold Standard:**
    
    The primary goal was to end the use of fiat currency, which was not backed by gold or silver, and return to a gold-backed currency system. 

    
    **Redemption of Greenbacks:**
    
    The act mandated that the government would redeem greenbacks with gold, making them convertible to a fixed gold equivalent. 

    
    **Retirement of Fractional Paper Currency:*
    
    It also called for the replacement of fractional paper currency with silver coins. 

    
    **Contraction of Money Supply:**
    
    Critics argued that the act's contraction of the money supply contributed to the [Long Depression](https://www.google.com/search?client=opera-gx&hs=ZyL&sca_esv=c16ee7d0133e54f0&sxsrf=AHTn8zrqiJDivh1sHrHQqq24jVHxe7M30Q%3A1746136608946&q=Long+Depression&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwil1LjioYONAxWSAHkGHWGDD-oQxccNegQIchAB&mstk=AUtExfDAOQxJbU3fFEG7MDn2oiVQxiARvwy7SlhNYCi8dqOqW-TAuKox8IWJEWupHJs5ivHKB7IBEKCP1wi6rhKOOn9b1biA1lQuZDWDWnfRxrtoLHkWBMkcx7WG1swLaMR24Nvc94QHfN4Ajnbn81lFDHp83RgE-08lVOLBNl1RwzPzFK0ZJj0FRxlc4BJWpI_524hILiDGokh4RW8n2fytnvnh1sY8E83r-9MckzdtYh46ZYXfp5lWkMa04Nukoh0vdGZMqJ_dVwsWVbLF44oK0RVs&csui=3) of the 1870s, [according to Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specie_Payment_Resumption_Act). 
    

Background and Context:

- **Post-Civil War Era:**
    
    The act was enacted in the aftermath of the Civil War, during which the government issued greenbacks to finance the war effort. 
    

- **Economic Instability:**
    
    The Civil War and its aftermath caused significant economic disruption, leading to inflation and concerns about the stability of the currency. 
    

- **Political Divisions:**
    
    The act sparked considerable political debate, with proponents arguing for economic stability and opponents worried about the potential for deflation.
