
 - African American Sufferage
- Passed by Congress, the fifteenth Amendment to the [[Constitution]] providing the "right of citizens of the [[United States]] to vote shall not be denied or abridged by the United States or  any State on account of race, color, or previous condition of servitude" 

- Extends the ideas created by the [[fourteenth Amendment]].
- Though ultimately a boon to African American men, the amendments were a bitter blow to [[Women's rights]] advocates.
