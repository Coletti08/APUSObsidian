
- The debates over Imperialism began mainly due to the fact that there is no more "American Frontier". However, the idea of [[Manifest Destiny]] still drove the country to expand in search of a "new frontier".
	- These discussions of a "new frontier" began with Fredric Jackson Turner who proposed the idea back in 1890 with his [[Frontier Thesis]].  

- 1899 marks the beginning of US Imperialism over seas, with the conclusion of the [[Spanish-American War]], and the [[1898 Treaty of Paris]] which ceded [Guam, Puerto Rico,] and the purchasing of the [Philippines] America starts to show signs of being an imperialist nation. 

- This sparks controversy and debate in the early 20th century. 


- There are two sides to this debate:
	- pro-Imperialists:
		- [[Teddy Roosevelt]] – advocated a strong navy and American global leadership
		- **Senator Henry Cabot Lodge** – promoted expansion as necessary for U.S. power
		- **Alfred Thayer Mahan** – argued naval power was key to global dominance
	- They advocated for new market expansion in other nations as well as greater access to resources
