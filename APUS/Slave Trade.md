
- Trans Atlantic Slave Trade is characterized as the "forced migration from Africa to the Americas", usually along the [[Middle Passage]]
-  Far more of these enslaved people went to places like the Caribbean or the West Indies, as Native Americans in southern America were used for slave labor there, and the slave trade in the Northern America didn't pick up until the introduction of [[Cash Crops]] in the 1700s. 

- The Slave Trade began in the middle of the fifteenth century when Portuguese ships sailed down the West African coast.
	- Their intention was to trade for gold and spices, but the reality was that "Human Commodities were more valuable".
