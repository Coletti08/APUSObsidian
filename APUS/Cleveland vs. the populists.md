
- [[Grover Cleveland]] who was elected the 24th president of the Untied states in part thanks to the [[Populist Party]], siphoned off some of the strength of the [[Republican]] party, by a vigorous campaign against the extravagance of the Republican "Billion Dollar Congress". 
	- However, in his second term Cleveland sought to keep the country on the [[Gold Standard]] and convinced Congress to enact an income tax (declared unconstitutional by Supreme Court). 
- by 1896, Cleveland's policies were repudiated by his own party.
