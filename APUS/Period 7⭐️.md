Key Ideas:
1. Growth expanding opportunity, while economic instability led to new efforts to reform U.S. society and its economic system.

2. Innovations in communications and technology contribute to the growth of mass culture, while significant changes occurred in internal and international migration patterns. 

3. Participation in a series of global conflicts propelled the United States into a position of international power while renewing domestic debates over the nation's proper role in the world. 

- This time was marked by an increasingly pluralistic [[United States]] that faced profound domestic and global challenges, debated the proper degree of government activism, and sought to define its international role. 

- New Century New America:
		- New foreign policy in the 1890's that included imperialism and intruded upon neighboring nations. 
		- US proposes to take over several island nations.
		- Constructs the [[Panama Canal]]
		- leverages international currency to exert power over economically weak nations.
	- the [[Cuban Independence]] struggle in the 1890's attracted US interest.
		- The sinking of the [[USS Maine]], in Havana Harbor which was exaggerated by the [[New York Journal]] placed much of the blame on Spain, and two months later there was a Declaration of War on Spain. 
	- This war prompted the US to expand its foreign policy. 
	- The war in Cuba prompted the US to begin an offense against the Spanish colony in the Philippines 
	- Ap provision of the [[1898 Treaty of Paris]] ended the [[Spanish-American War]], ceded the Philippines to the US, 
		- US occupation of the Philippines would bring up many debates over the ethics of Imperialism. 
	- Another aspect of this debate had to do with Latin America, the us wanted more goods and economic opportunities, so the Rosevelt Administration began construction of the [[Panama Canal]].
	- [[Teddy Roosevelt]] devised the [[Roosevelt Corollary to the  Monroe Doctrine]], as part of his state of the union address in 1904. Reminding the people of the United States of their role as an "International police power". 
		- Committed the US to ending European incursion into Latin American states and nations.
		- The Corollary was used as justification for the US to move into island nations such as: Cuba, Nicaragua, Haiti, and the Dominican Republic. 
		- This is known as [[Big Stick Politics]] .
	- Big stick politics used on a international economic level through [[Dollar Diplomacy]]. 
- The emerging global superpower:
	- At the end of [[Reconstruction]], [[Adolf Hitler]] began his campaign in Europe, bringing the world to the brink of global conflict
		- At first European powers attempted to appeased Germany, and when [[Appeasement]] failed the world descended into [[WWII]]. 
	- US stayed out of WWII for a while due to the lasting economic crisis brought about by the [[Great Depression]] thanks to the [[Neutrality Act of 1939]]. 
	- In 1941 America tested their own policy by releasing the [[Lend-Lease Act]], which kept America out of direct action, but allowed them to transfer or "Lease" weapons, ammunition, and motor vehicles to nations who needed them. 
	- That is until the attack on [[Pearl Harbor]] in 1941, which brought the US officially into the world war. 
		- The war consumed all aspects of American life, from laborers and women working in the factories, to the people fighting on the front lines the war through propaganda and government facilitation took over all aspects of life. 
	- The idea of [[Victory Gardens]] arrises. 
	- The attack on Pearl Harbor lead to a deep hatred towards all things Japanese, this is the beginning of [[Japanese Internment]] or [Executive order 9066], which allowed the US to separate and intern all Japanese citizens. 
	- Truman orders the bombing of [[Hiroshima]] and [[Nagasaki]] which ushered the world into an era where wide spread destruction was possible.
