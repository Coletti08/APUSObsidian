
- in June of 1898, the Filipino general, [Emilio Aguinaldo] proclaimed Philippine independence and established Asia's first republic. 
- Aguinaldo had hoped that the US would help the Philippines out, but president [[William McKinley]] had a lot of pressure to annex the country. 
- Then in Feb, 1899 fighting began between American and Filipino soldiers. 
- Thanks to the adoption of [[Gorilla Tactics]] the Filipinos were able to defeat the US.
