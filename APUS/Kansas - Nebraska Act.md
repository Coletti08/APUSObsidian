
- Changed the status of slavery in a region from [[Missouri Compromise]]'s 36° 30' N. to a vote from the region's settlers known as *Popular Sovereignty*.
- Directly lead to [[Bleeding Kansas]] (1855 - 1860)

- Enacted due to the need to construct the [[Trans Continental Railroad]]. 

- Senator Stephan A. Douglas proposed that the area west of Iowa and Missouri (Indian Land), become free for white settlement. 
- The only condition from southern congress members was that the [[Missouri Compromise]] be null and void. 
- Douglas's bill created two territories: Kansas and Nebraska. 

- Exposed the Failure of Popular Sovereignty
	- The violence showed that letting states decide slavery through voting wouldn’t work peacefully
	- *It discredited Stephen Douglas’s compromise strategy.
