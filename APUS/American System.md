

-- created by [[Henry Clay]] The American System was an economic policy of internal improvements for transportation and agriculture funded by vacation; a protectionist tariff on behalf of American industry; and preservation of a [[National Bank]]. 


Comprised of Three Parts:
- **[[National Bank]]:**
    
    Clay believed a national bank was crucial for a stable currency and credit system, which would support economic activity across the nation.
    
- **Protective Tariffs:**
    
    He advocated for tariffs on imported goods to protect American manufacturers from foreign competition and encourage domestic production.
    
- **Internal Improvements:**

    Clay championed federal funding for infrastructure projects like canals, roads, and bridges, arguing they would improve transportation, connect markets, and facilitate trade, especially between the agricultural South and the industrial North, as well as the burgeoning West.
