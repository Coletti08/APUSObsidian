
- One of the main issues addressed by the [[Progressive Movement]] was labor conditions, especially for children. 

- [[Muckraking]] journalism and action from social and labor activists led to the formation of the National Labor Committee in 1904. 

- As a part of their charge, the committee investigated labor conditions around the nations. 
	- Despite their efforts the first real effective labor legislation wasn't passed for another 30 years, being passed during the [[New Deal]].
