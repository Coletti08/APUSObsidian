- Scott held as a slave in Missouri in 1846, sued for his freedom arguing that he lived for four years in Illinois, a free state, and Wisconsin, a free territory. 
	- Claimed that his residence on free soil had erased his slave status.

	- Also claimed that the [[Missouri Compromise]] was unconstitutional; and that congress had no right to exclude slavery from the territories.
