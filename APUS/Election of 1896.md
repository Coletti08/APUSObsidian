
- The election between the [[Republican]] [[William McKinley]], and the [[Democratic]] [[Populist Party]] candidate [[William Jennings Bryan]]. 

- This was another major loss for populists who wanted economic change in America, as McKinley supported the coin of "sound money", which shows the beneficial aspects of the republicans protectionist policy compared to the detrimental impact of the Democratic free trade policy. 

- The election debated the [[Gold Standard]] versus the [[Silver Standard]]. 
- The **Election of 1896** was a watershed moment that redefined American politics. It resolved key economic debates, reshaped party identities, and introduced innovative campaign strategies. The outcomes of this election influenced the nation's political trajectory well into the 20th century.
