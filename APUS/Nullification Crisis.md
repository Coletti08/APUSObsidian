
- Started in 1832 when South Carolina vice president [John C. Calhoun], in protest of federal tariffs ([[Tariff of 1828]] and [[Tariff of 1832]]) 

- Calhoun adopted an [[Ordinance of nullification]] declaring the tariffs of 1828, and 1832 null and void in the state of South Carolina. 

- [[Andrew Jackson]] charged the state was "on the brink of insurrection and treason". Jackson sent federal military forces to Charleston, but the crisis was resolved with the passage of [[Henry Clay]]'s [[Compromise Tariff of 1833]].
