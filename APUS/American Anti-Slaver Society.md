
- Pledged "immediate emancipation with out expatriation" 
- in contrast to [[American Colonization Society]], which had been founded in 1816 with the goal of relocating freed slaves to land purchased in West Africa.
- Many abolitionists opposed colonization, arguing that its aim was to remove free blacks from America rather then end slavery itself. 
- Before the end of the [[Civil War]], the ACS settled around 10,000 black Americans in Africa.
