
- The papal Bull "Inter Caetera", was issued by Pope Alexander VI on May 4th, 1493. 
- It played a central role in the Spanish conquest of the [[New World]]. 
	- Outlined Spain's strategy to ensure its exclusive right to the lands discovered by [[Christopher Columbus]] in 1492. 
	- Created a line from one hundred leagues west pf Azores and Cape Verde, and assigned all of that land to Spain. 
		- Territorial possession over this land
		- Trade with any inhabitance within this area
- Stated that "any land not inhabited by Christians was available to be discovered, claimed, and exploited". 
- This is referenced in the [[Doctrine of Discovery]] which was became the basis of all European claims over the New World. 

- This sentiment remained for along time as it was referenced in [[Johnson v. McIntosh]]
