
- After Cuba began to rebel against Spain in 1895, Newspapers like "The New Yorker" and publishers such as [William Randolph Hearst] and [Joseph Pulitzer Pulitzer] worked up war fever among the public with reports of Spanish atrocities against Cuban Rebels. 

- On Feb 15, 1898 an explosion sank the  [[USS Maine]] in Havana harbor. 10 days later, Congress declared war, but only after adopting the [[Teller Amendment]], which made it clear that it didn't harbor imperialist ambitions.
